## Why Forge TV

- **Built for the remote.** Every screen is designed for a TV: clear focus, one press to watch, and a guide that feels like a real set-top box.
- **Quick, even on slow boxes.** The last channel starts as soon as the app opens, channels start on the first decodable frame, the guide opens straight away from what is saved on the TV, and it runs on TVs as old as Android 6.
- **Everything stays on your TV.** Playlists, logins, favourites and guide data are stored on the device, and each channel plays straight from your provider.

## Features

### 📺 Live TV and guide
- Time-grid guide with a "now" line, or a simpler Now and next view.
- Categories, favourites, recently watched, and one Search for channels, programmes, movies and shows that keeps up as you type.
- **Voice search:** press the microphone key on the remote and say a channel or programme.
- Programme listings from your playlist's XMLTV guide, refreshed in the background. A **guide time offset** fixes listings that are in the wrong time zone.
- Number keys, Channel ±, Last channel and an info banner, just like a normal TV.
- Rename, hide, reorder and lock channels and categories, and keep those changes when the playlist refreshes. While moving something, Right sends it to the top of the list and Left to the bottom.
- Channel logos sit straight on the dark guide, with no white plate behind them.
- Very large playlists, with tens of thousands of channels, load and save reliably.

### ✨ Make it yours
- **Tidy names:** take tags such as "UK |", "[VIP]" or "FHD" off channel, category, movie and show names. Forge lists the tags your playlist uses most, and you can add your own. A tag only comes off as a whole word, so "UK" leaves "UKTV" alone.
- **Your own groups:** gather channels from any category, and from any of your playlists, into a group of your own, such as the ones the family watches. A group shows in the guide after Recently watched whichever playlist is on, and each channel plays from its own playlist with that playlist's listings. Each profile has its own groups, and you can reorder, rename or delete them.
- **Second guide:** add another XMLTV guide to a playlist to fill in the channels its own guide has no listings for.
- **Choose guide channel:** when a channel shows the wrong listings, pick its guide channel by hand from a list of close matches, or go back to matching automatically.
- **Menu opacity:** five steps from See-through to Solid for popups, dialogs and the player menu (Settings › Playback › Picture).
- **Remote buttons:** give the red, green, yellow and blue buttons, or any other button you add, a new job while watching live TV, such as Last channel, Favourites or the sleep timer.

### ⏪ Catch-up and reminders
- **Catch-up:** programmes that have already been on are marked in the guide, and play from the start, on channels whose provider keeps an archive.
- **Start over:** missed the beginning? Play the programme that's on now from the start.
- **Reminders:** choose Remind me on a programme coming up, and the TV prompts you when it starts.

### 🏠 Home screen
- What's on now beside a live preview, with one press to watch.
- Continue watching, Favourite channels and Recently watched rows, ready as soon as the app opens.
- **Recommended series** and **Recommended movies**, picked from what each profile watches: its favourite categories and genres, and the next part of a film series it has started. Titles you've already seen are left out.

### 🏟 Sports tab
- **Every game, and where to watch it:** pick a sport, then a league, to see its games for the next week, day by day, with kick-off times or live scores and how many of your channels are showing each one.
- Press OK on a game to list those channels. One that's on now starts straight away; one still to come opens the guide at that programme.
- **Favourites:** hold OK on a league or a game to follow the league or either team. **My favourites** then lists all their games together. Each profile keeps its own.
- **Preferred channels:** rank the channels you like best, and OK on a game that is live tunes the highest-ranked one showing it. Games still to come keep their reminder menu, and Choose a channel lists every channel.
- **Reminders for games:** hold OK on an upcoming game and choose Remind me on the channel you want, and the TV prompts you when it starts.
- Covers football, basketball, American football, baseball, ice hockey, rugby, tennis, golf, motor racing, cricket and more. Darts, snooker, boxing and other sports without live scores list the guide's programmes for that sport instead.

### ⚽ Live sports screen
- When a match is on, the picture shrinks to make room for live scores, scorers and the rest of the league.
- Statistics along the bottom that suit the sport: possession and shots for football, rebounds and assists for basketball, yards for American football, and the score for each quarter, period or innings.
- A **Detailed** or **Compact** scoreboard, and you can browse the other matches for their scorers and statistics without changing channel.
- **Jump to any game:** while browsing the scores, hold OK on a match to list the channels showing it, and pick one to switch. Hold OK on the game you're watching to move to another channel showing it.
- The how-to hints show when the sports screen opens and briefly after a change, then get out of the way.
- **Score delay** holds results back so the scores don't spoil what you haven't seen yet. Tune it with the remote, or let Auto follow the stream.
- Football across the UK, Europe and beyond, plus basketball, American football, baseball, ice hockey, rugby, tennis, golf, motor racing, cricket and more.

### 🎬 Movies and TV shows
- Your provider's library as rows of posters: recently added, new releases, top rated and every category. Rows wrap round, and each category appears once, in full.
- **A proper page for every title:** the title's own logo over its backdrop, age rating and picture quality, cast, director, release date and country, and a **Trailer** button when the provider has one.
- A show opens on its own page with its seasons and episode count, and one button carries on from the next episode. The episodes, laid out as widescreen stills with your progress on each, are one press away.
- Titles, episodes and Continue watching open straight away, even with a library of a hundred thousand titles.
- When the provider has no synopsis, Forge fills one in from **TVmaze** or **Wikipedia**.
- **Skip intro** on episodes whose intro has been timed, and **Up next** counts down to the next episode over the credits.
- The banner shows when a title ends and what's next, with buttons to skip back 10 s, pause, skip ahead 30 s or go to the next episode.
- **Trailers play inside the app**, with OK to pause and Back to return to the title page.
- **TMDB's rating**, labelled, and the **age rating for the country you choose** in Settings.
- **Mark watched or unwatched** from a title page, the hold menu or Continue watching, and switch on **Unwatched only** to hide finished movies and episodes.
- Resume or start over from Continue watching.

### 🪟 Picture in picture and multiscreen
- **Picture in picture:** the Picture in picture icon in the player options bar picks a playlist and channel for a small second picture over live TV, movies and episodes. One picture has the sound, and you can switch it.
- **Multiscreen:** show 2, 3 or 4 live channels at once, side by side, one large and the rest small, or 2 by 2. The D-pad moves between screens and OK opens a screen's menu to pick a channel from any playlist that allows it, give it the sound, watch it full screen or close it.
- Each playlist can be left out of it, and can record how many streams its account allows. Forge warns you when pictures share an account that allows fewer.

### 🔊 Audio and subtitles
- Pick subtitles and audio from a clear list: each track is named by language, layout and format (for example *English · 5.1 · Dolby Digital*), with subtitles for the deaf and hard of hearing (SDH) and audio description marked. The remote's subtitle and audio keys open the lists directly.
- Choices made in a film are kept for that film, and for a series they are kept for every episode.
- **Audio sync** moves the sound earlier or later, in small steps, when voices and lips don't match. It is kept for the TV, since it usually comes from the soundbar.
- **Playback speed** from 0.5× to 2× for films, episodes and catch-up, with voices keeping their pitch.
- Preferred subtitle and audio languages, a preference for SDH subtitles or audio description, and foreign-language subtitles that still show when subtitles are off.
- Ready-made subtitle looks: Standard, **High contrast** (large yellow words on a solid box), **Large print** and Outline only.
- **Dolby and DTS to a soundbar:** Auto, Off (always decode on the TV) or Always (always pass through).

### 📱 Phone companion
- Pair a phone by scanning a QR code on the TV. There is no app to install; it opens in the phone's browser.
- Add playlists, change settings, reorder favourites and show or hide categories from the phone instead of typing with the remote.
- Pairing uses a code shown only on the TV screen, and paired phones can be unlinked at any time.
- The subtitle and audio description options can be set from the phone as well.

### 💾 Backup and restore
- One file holds your playlists and logins, settings, favourites, channel changes, profiles and Continue watching.
- Restore it on the same TV or a new one, from a USB stick or a cloud drive, or through the phone companion.
- **Safe restores:** the whole file is checked before anything on the TV changes, so a damaged backup is turned away rather than half restored. If the TV is switched off mid-restore, Forge finishes or undoes it the next time it opens.

### 🖐 Touch, mouse, VR and TalkBack
- Works on phones and tablets by touch: swipe to change channel, brightness and volume, pinch to fill the screen.
- Live TV plays smoothly on phones, using the phone's hardware decoder first and keeping Wi-Fi awake while you watch.
- Runs on Meta Quest, Android XR and Pico headsets, where the controller trigger or a hand pinch acts as a tap.
- With **TalkBack** on, the guide reads out the channel, programme and times as you move, forms read their labels and errors, and the player's buttons and track changes are spoken.

### 👪 Profiles and parental controls
- Profiles each keep their own favourites, recently watched and Continue watching.
- Optional "Who's watching?" when the app opens.
- A parental PIN locks whole categories out of the guide, search and channel changes.

### ⚡ Playback that keeps going
- Tuned for quick channel changes, with a choice of start-up buffer (Fast, Balanced or Steady). The guide stays quick on slow TVs too.
- **The last channel starts as soon as the app opens**, with what's on now and next shown before the guide has loaded.
- A dropped stream reconnects by itself, and straight away when the network comes back. A paused film stays paused, and keeps its playback speed, when it reconnects.
- **MPEG-TS or HLS, per playlist:** each Xtream Codes playlist has its own live stream type, so one provider can use HLS while another stays on MPEG-TS. A playlist link that asks for HLS starts on HLS.
- **Match frame rate** switches the TV to suit the channel, such as 50 Hz broadcasts on a 60 Hz box.
- Picture size (Fit, Zoom or Stretch) for everything or per channel, and subtitles styled your way: size, colour, background and position.
- Playback details on screen (formats, decoder, dropped frames, buffer) for diagnosing jerky video.
- **Sleep timer** from 15 minutes to 2 hours, with a minute's warning to keep watching.
- **Open when the TV starts**, so Forge is the first thing on screen.
- **Open in another app** hands a channel or title to another video player on the device.

## Install

1. Download the APK from [forgetv.app](https://forgetv.app/), or from the newest release on the [Releases page](https://github.com/tomdonegan/forge-standalone-releases/releases) (`forge-tv-standalone-<version>.apk`).
2. Get the APK onto your device, for example with a downloader app on the TV, a USB stick, or `adb install forge-tv-standalone-<version>.apk` from a computer.
3. Allow your device to install apps from that source when Android asks ("Install unknown apps").
4. Open Forge TV and add your playlist: an M3U/M3U8 link, an M3U file, or an Xtream Codes login. You can type it on the TV or send it from your phone.

### Updates

Forge TV checks this page for new versions a few seconds after it opens and offers **Update now**, **Later** or **Skip this version**. Updates download and install from inside the app, so you only sideload once. Every release is signed with the same Forge TV key, so it installs over the one you have and keeps your playlists, profiles and settings. The check can be turned off, or run by hand, in **Settings › About**.

New to the remote? The [how-to guide](https://forgetv.app/how-to.html) covers the buttons, the guide, the Sports tab and the player in a few short sections.

## Supported devices

- Android TV and Google TV boxes, sticks and televisions running **Android 6.0 or later**.
- Android phones and tablets, with touch and mouse.
- Meta Quest, Android XR and Pico headsets, where the app runs in a panel.

## Bring your own playlist

Forge TV is a player only. **It does not include, sell or provide any channels, streams or content.** You need your own M3U playlist or Xtream Codes login from a service you are entitled to use, and you are responsible for what you play with it.
