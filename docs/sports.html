Forge TV / Live sports

Live sports

Follow live sports while you watch

Forge TV combines a sports schedule, channel listings and a live match information view so you can keep an eye on fixtures without abandoning the television.

Find matches and their channels

Open the Sports tab to browse games by sport, league and day. Forge TV can show which channels in your own playlist are listed for a match, helping you find a broadcast without manually searching every channel.

Scores, goalscorers and statistics

While watching a match, the optional sports screen can display scores, goalscorers and available statistics next to the stream. Browse other games without changing channels; after a period of inactivity the view returns to the match you're watching.

Follow favourite teams and leagues

My favourites

Follow a team or league and bring their matches together for easier browsing.

Upcoming match reminders

Set a reminder for a game listed in the guide, then return when the programme is about to start.

Score delay

Adjust the score delay and spoiler controls if the live data is ahead of your TV stream.

Compact scoreboards

Choose a compact or detailed scoreboard presentation to fit the space alongside the stream.

Remote-control shortcuts

From the Sports tab, press OK on a match to see channels associated with it. While the sports overlay is showing, use the directional controls to browse games. The remote-control guide explains the full navigation and available alternatives to coloured remote buttons.

Availability varies: Scores, statistics, channel matches and reminders depend on available sports data, guide listings, your playlist and network connectivity. Forge TV does not provide sporting broadcasts.
