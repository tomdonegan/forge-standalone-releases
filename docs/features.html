Forge TV / Features

What Forge TV offers

Explore Forge TV's features

Built around a TV remote, Forge TV brings live channels, a readable programme guide, a movie library and match information into one Android app.

Live TV and programme guide

A proper TV guide

Browse channels in a time-grid guide or now-and-next view, see programme details and start watching with your remote.

Make channels yours

Create custom groups, mark favourites, tidy channel names and choose guide listings when your playlist data needs adjusting.

Live sports alongside your stream

Browse upcoming matches and the channels listing them, follow teams and leagues, set reminders, and view scores, goalscorers and match statistics beside a live stream when data is available. Learn more on the live sports features page.

Movies, TV shows and continue watching

Browse by artwork

Explore films and TV shows with poster rows, details, trailers where available and recommendations.

Pick up where you left off

Resume films and shows, see episode progress, and use Skip intro and Up next features during playback.

Settings that fit the way you watch

Playback options

Adjust playback buffer, select MPEG-TS or HLS per playlist, manage subtitles and audio tracks, and configure picture size.

Profiles and parental controls

Keep favourites and continue-watching progress separate for each profile and use a PIN to restrict categories.

Backup and remote controls

Save your settings in one backup file, customise remote shortcuts and manage compatible devices more easily.

Bring your own content. Forge TV is a playback app, not an IPTV subscription provider. It contains no channels or streams. Catch-up and Start Over depend on your playlist provider.
