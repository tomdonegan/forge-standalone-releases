What Forge TV offers

Everything Forge TV can do

Built around a TV remote, Forge TV brings live channels, a readable programme guide, a movie library and match information into one Android app. Jump to a section below, or download it and try it with your own playlist.

Live TV and programme guide

  • Time-grid guide with a now line, or a simpler Now and next view
  • Categories, favourites and recently watched, plus one search for channels, programmes, movies and shows
  • Voice search: press the microphone key on the remote and say a channel or programme
  • Guide listings from your playlist's XMLTV guide, a second guide for channels it misses, and a time offset for listings in the wrong time zone
  • Choose the guide channel by hand when a channel shows the wrong listings
  • Number keys, Channel up and down, Last channel and an info banner, like a normal TV
  • Catch-up and Start over on channels whose provider keeps an archive
  • Programme reminders that prompt you when a show is about to start, with the option to change channel for you
  • Very large playlists with tens of thousands of channels

Home screen

  • Opens on Home, with the channel you were watching beside a live preview and what is on now
  • Continue watching for movies and episodes, with progress, resume, start over and remove
  • Favourite channels and Recently watched rows
  • Recommended series and movies based on what each profile watches

Movies and TV shows

  • Your provider's library as rows of posters: recently added, new releases, top rated and every category
  • A full page for each title with logo, backdrop, age rating, picture quality, cast, director and a Trailer button when available
  • TV shows open on a page with seasons and a single button that carries on from the next episode
  • Skip intro on episodes where the intro has been timed, and Up next over the credits
  • Mark watched or unwatched, with an Unwatched only switch to hide finished titles
  • Missing synopses filled in from TVmaze or Wikipedia, and TMDB ratings labelled as such
  • Tidy names strips provider tags such as UK |, [VIP] and release labels from titles

Sports

  • Sports tab listing each sport's games for the next week, with the channels in your guide showing each one
  • Follow leagues and teams, and see them together under My favourites
  • Rank preferred channels so a live game tunes the best one automatically
  • Game reminders that prompt you at the start, 5 or 15 minutes before
  • A live sports screen that shrinks the picture to show scores, scorers and match statistics beside a channel
  • Score delay so results don't spoil what you haven't seen yet
  • Jump to another channel showing the same game

Playlists and sources

  • M3U or M3U8 links and files, Xtream Codes logins and Stalker (Ministra, MAG) portals
  • Several playlists at once, each with channel count, last update and a status light showing whether the server answers
  • Xtream logins are checked before they are saved
  • Choose a default playlist for Live TV and another for movies and shows
  • Your own groups can gather channels from every playlist
  • Rename, hide, reorder and lock channels and categories, and keep those changes when a playlist refreshes
  • Live stream type, MPEG-TS or HLS, set for each playlist

Player controls

  • Hold Down on live TV, or Down or hold OK on a movie or episode, to open the player options bar
  • Audio and subtitle track menus with plain names, remembered per film and per series
  • Audio sync, playback speed from 0.5x to 2x, and preferred audio and subtitle languages
  • Subtitle looks including High contrast and Large print, with size, colour and background
  • Picture size: Fit, Zoom or Stretch, for everything or per channel
  • Picture in picture and multiscreen with up to four live channels
  • Match frame rate, a sleep timer and playback details for diagnosing jerky video
  • Open a channel in another player such as VLC or MX Player
  • Dolby and DTS passed to a soundbar, or decoded on the TV
  • Fast channel changes, automatic reconnection when a stream drops, and a last channel that starts as soon as the app opens

Sync and backup

  • Sync between TVs through one encrypted file in your own storage: Dropbox (in a private app folder) or a WebDAV server such as Nextcloud or a NAS
  • The file is locked with your passphrase before it leaves the TV, so the server never sees your playlist logins. A recovery key is shown once at set-up
  • Sync when the app opens, or Sync now. Changes from another device are only taken when nothing is playing
  • Backup to one file with playlists, settings, favourites, channel changes, profiles and Continue watching
  • Restores are checked in full before anything changes, and an interrupted restore is finished or undone on next start

Remote and phone companion

  • Pair a phone by scanning a QR code on the TV. Nothing to install: it opens in the phone's browser
  • Add playlists, change settings, reorder favourites and show or hide categories from the phone
  • Pairing uses a code shown only on the TV, and paired phones can be unlinked at any time
  • Remote buttons: give the red, green, yellow and blue buttons, or any button you press, a job on live TV
  • Works on phones, tablets and VR headsets by touch, mouse or pointer
  • TalkBack support throughout the guide, forms and player

Customisation and profiles

  • Profiles with their own favourites, recently watched and Continue watching, with an optional Who's watching? at start-up
  • Parental PIN that locks categories out of the guide, search and channel changes
  • Icon or word menus, guide channels as logo, name or both, and five steps of menu opacity
  • Open the app when the TV starts, and give the TV a custom name
  • Live TV can open on a chosen category

Privacy and updates

  • Playlists, logins, favourites and guide data stay on your TV, and channels play straight from your provider
  • Forge TV runs no servers for your data and needs no account
  • The parental PIN is stored as a salted hash
  • Updates are offered inside the app. Releases are signed with the same key, so they install over your current version and keep your playlists, profiles and settings
  • Forge TV is a player only. It contains no channels or streams
Bring your own content. Forge TV is a playback app, not an IPTV subscription provider. It contains no channels or streams. Catch-up and Start Over depend on your playlist provider.
