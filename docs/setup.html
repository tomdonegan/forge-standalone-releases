Forge TV / Playlist setup

First-time setup

Set up your M3U or Xtream Codes playlist

Forge TV is a player for playlists you already have permission to use. You can add an M3U link, import an M3U file or sign in using Xtream Codes credentials.

Before you start: Forge TV does not sell or supply IPTV subscriptions, channels or content. You need playlist information from a legitimate service you are entitled to access. Never share your login credentials in public feedback or support posts.

Add your playlist

  1. Install and open Forge TV

    Get the APK from the official download page and launch Forge TV on your device.

  2. Choose the playlist format

    Use an M3U URL or M3U file if that's what your provider offers, or Xtream Codes login details if provided.

  3. Enter your playlist information

    Type the details using the TV remote or use the phone companion to send them from your phone's browser, where available.

  4. Let the channels and guide load

    Once imported, open Live TV to see your channels. Electronic programme guide coverage depends on the information your playlist and guide sources provide.

M3U vs Xtream Codes

M3U playlist

Typically a playlist URL or a file containing channel stream entries. Choose this method when you have a valid M3U link or file.

Xtream Codes

Uses connection details and credentials supplied by your service. Enter those in the Xtream Codes playlist setup option.

If channels do not start or keep stopping

Open Settings → Playlists, choose the affected playlist, and try switching its Live stream type to HLS. Forge TV allows playback type to be configured per playlist. Stream availability and quality still depend on the source, device and network.

If guide times look wrong

The guide has settings for its display and time offset. Open Settings → Guide to adjust them and confirm that your playlist provides suitable listings.

Moving to another TV?

Use Settings → Backup to save your setup, preferences and playlists to a file, then restore it on the other device. Store the backup securely because it can contain playlist credentials.
